What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that learns patterns from data and then creates original content. It is useful in multimedia projects because it helps students and creators produce ideas and media faster.

Input: Prompt, image, sound, or text
->
AI Model: Learns patterns and generates content
->
Output: New image, video clip, voice, or article

Pros & Cons of Generative AI

Balanced view of opportunities and challenges for real-world projects.

Advantages

  • Fast content creation for images, scripts, and voice.
  • Lower cost and time in multimedia production.
  • Supports creativity and rapid idea testing.
  • Useful for students, designers, and content teams.

Disadvantages

  • Can produce incorrect or biased results.
  • Raises copyright and originality concerns.
  • Needs human review before final publishing.
  • Quality depends on prompt clarity and data quality.
